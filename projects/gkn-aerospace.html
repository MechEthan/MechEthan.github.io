Role Overview

Overview

From Manufacturing to Quality

During my internship with GKN Aerospace, I supported engineering activities within an aerospace composite manufacturing facility. My work initially focused on manufacturing improvement before shifting toward quality and process improvement.

This gave me hands-on exposure to composite layup, tooling, curing processes, defect analysis, and production documentation. I also applied Lean, Six Sigma, RCCA, MRB, and GD&T concepts while working with engineers, quality personnel, and production operators.

Technical Experience

Project Highlight
DWG E-01.1

Recovering Scrapped Parts

One of my largest projects involved reviewing parts that had previously been identified as scrap. By working through the applicable requirements and quality review process, I helped identify parts that could be recovered rather than discarded.

The effort ultimately supported the recovery of approximately 360 parts, representing roughly $2.5 million in material value. The project demonstrated how engineering review and process knowledge can turn previously lost material into usable product.

Engineering in Practice

Process

More Than the Technical Solution

The internship also showed me how engineering decisions affect the people working directly with a process. I worked with operators and quality personnel to improve how parts were tracked and communicated, helping make the purpose of the improvement effort clearer to the team.

This experience reinforced that an effective engineering solution has to work technically while also fitting into an existing process and being practical for the people who use it every day.

Takeaway

My First Aerospace Engineering Experience

GKN strengthened my interest in aerospace engineering, advanced manufacturing, and hands-on problem solving. More importantly, it gave me my first experience seeing how mechanical engineering principles, manufacturing processes, quality systems, and people come together to solve real production problems.
