Smart Structures & Microtransducers

Graduate Coursework · NC State University

The goal of this project was to assess existing smart structure technology in a certain field and determine a potential gap or problem in the indusrty. Then our team had to develope a possibe solution and prove it's feasability. My team looked at Structural Health Monitoring, the continous, real-time assessment of engineered structures. We discoved that in extreme conditions, primarily temperatures, there are numerious issues with standard CMUT designs.

CMUT Size Analysis

CMUT Material Analysis

Goals

  • Research Current Technologies
  • Determine a Gap/Problem
  • Develope a Solution

Design Approach

Standard CMUT technology uses temerature compensated relationship to allow a voltage to be biased based on the measured temperature and changes in the rffective stiffness of the membrane. To improve at high temperature we considered adaptive bias control, material changes, and smaller sizes.

Results

Using a titanium membrane with a diameter oof 80 micrometers decreased displacemnt 40% and stress by 20%
